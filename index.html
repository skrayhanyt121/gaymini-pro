Combat Arts

Legendary Jutsu & Powers

Shinobi harness spiritual and physical energy (Chakra) to perform superhuman techniques ranging from destructive elemental jutsus to mind-bending illusions and space-time ninjutsu.

Rasengan

A swirling sphere of chakra formed in the palm of the user's hand, created by Minato Namikaze and perfected by Naruto.

Sharingan & Mangekyō

The legendary dōjutsu bloodline limit of the Uchiha clan granting heightened perception, genjutsu mastery, and devastating ocular powers.

Sage Mode (Sennin Mōddo)

An empowered state achieved by blending natural energy with one's own chakra, multiplying physical strength and sensory awareness.

Konoha Shinobi Code

  • Never Give Up: The true essence of a ninja is endurance, resilience, and unyielding willpower (Guts).
  • Comrades Over Rules: Those who break the rules are scum, but those who abandon their comrades are worse than scum.
  • The Will of Fire: The belief that love and passion flow through the village like a burning fire, protecting future generations.

Chronicles of Glory

The Epic Sagas

From novice genin examinations to the war that saved humanity.

Stage 01

Land of Waves

Team 7's first major mission facing Zabuza Momochi and Haku, forging bonds of brotherhood and ninja resolve.

Stage 02

Chūnin Exams & Invasion

Elites from across nations gather. Orochimaru's invasion shakes Konoha to its core and introduces the legendary Sannin.

Stage 03

Pain's Assault

Akatsuki attacks Konoha. Naruto returns in Sage Mode to save the village and achieves true hero status.

Stage 04

Fourth Great Ninja War

The allied shinobi forces unite against Madara and Kaguya, culminating in Naruto and Sasuke's final fateful duel.
